What it does

One app instead of five.

Your notes, to-dos, calendar, contacts and passwords usually live in separate places. NOTEPANS keeps them together, linked, and searchable from one bar.

Capture

Notes, photos, GIFs and links, saved the moment you think of them.

Plan

Tasks, projects, events and shopping lists, each with due dates and progress.

Remember

Contacts, promises, locations and tags that tie it all together.

Protect

Passwords and sensitive notes, encrypted in your private vault.

  • NotesWith photos, GIFs, files or a location attached. PDF, Word and PowerPoint files open right inside the app.
  • TasksSubtasks, priorities and due dates. Progress is worked out from the checklist for you.
  • EventsA calendar with reminders, including ones that repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
  • VaultPasswords and secret notes, with a built-in password generator.
  • ContactsCall, text or copy their details in one tap.
  • PromisesThings you told someone you'd do, with reminders so they don't slip.
  • Locations & mapSave places, see them on a real map, and download an area for use without internet.
  • ProjectsBigger efforts with a visual Gantt timeline and their related tasks.
  • CollectionsGroup anything together your way: a trip, a client, a renovation.
  • SearchOne search bar across everything you've saved.
  • Backup & restoreOne encrypted file you control. Bring it back on a new phone.
  • Your lookTen colour themes and a little companion that lives on your Home screen.

Private by design

Your data never leaves your phone.

There's no server to upload to and no account to create. That's not a setting, it's how the app is built.

Encrypted on-device

Your database is encrypted with AES-256. Vault entries get a second layer from your master password.

No tracking

No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no ads. Android's cloud backup is switched off for the app on purpose.

Works offline

Everything runs without a connection. The only network use is loading map tiles, and only when you open the map.

Questions

Good to know before you test

What do I need to test it?

An Android phone with the Google Play Store, and a Gmail / Google account signed in to it. It takes about a minute to join.

Why does the app need testers at all?

Google requires new personal developer accounts to run a closed test with at least people for days before an app can be released to everyone. Every tester genuinely helps.

What does the app collect about me?

Nothing. Your notes, tasks, passwords and photos stay on your phone in an encrypted database. There's no account, no analytics and no ads. See the privacy policy for details.

What if I forget my master password?

Nobody can recover it, not even the developer, because nothing is stored anywhere else. That's what makes the vault private. Keep a backup file and remember the password you set.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Email the developer with what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help. Anything confusing is useful feedback too.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, anytime, right on your phone. See how to delete your data.
